# Woodland asset additions - 2026-10-27

Generated with the built-in image generation tool and copied into `public/textures/`. No source assets from the reference game were copied. These are albedo sources; the browser derives approximate normals and coverage-preserving mipmaps. Transparent cutouts preserve their returned alpha. The fern/litter prompts below record their generation intent; the remaining prompts are the exact production prompts.

## Leaf litter

Photorealistic seamless top-down forest-floor albedo: brown oak/beech leaves, thin broken twigs, granular soil and sparse irregular moss; neutral diffuse illumination without directional shadows or perspective. Fine natural debris, restrained colours and no large recognizable repeating objects.

## Fern

Photorealistic temperate woodland fern clump, entire plant within the frame, irregular arching fronds and fine pinnae, diffuse neutral illumination, transparent background and transparent gaps, no ground, text or pot. Suitable for crossed vegetation cards.

## Bracken

Create a photorealistic game vegetation cutout texture: a single natural clump of temperate European bracken, 5 irregular triangular fronds on thin branching brown-green stems, some olive green and some ochre dry tips, full plant including basal stems centered bottom, front view with mild three-quarter depth. Genuinely transparent background and transparent gaps between all leaflets. Whole silhouette inside frame with generous margin at top and sides. Fine detailed botanical pinnae, no pot, no ground, no text. Diffuse neutral unlit albedo lighting, no baked directional shadows or rim lighting. Designed for an alpha-tested cross-card plant, irregular asymmetric broad spreading crown, taller than a small fern. High resolution.

## Wild strawberry

An isolated low wild strawberry plant game sprite, photorealistic realistic fine leaves, fully transparent empty background (PNG alpha cutout). NO BACKGROUND SCENE, NO BOKEH, NO GREEN BLUR, NO GRADIENT. Ten serrated trifoliate dark green leaves on thin stems, two tiny white five-petal flowers. Small asymmetrical patch, full plant with stems reaching bottom centre. A clean cutout like a vegetation texture atlas element. Plant only. Entire plant fits within frame. Neutral diffuse lighting. Transparency everywhere outside leaves and stems including internal gaps.

A wood-sorrel generation and its attempted background-removal edit were rejected because they retained a blurred backdrop. Neither is shipped.

## Scree

Generate a seamless tileable photorealistic ground albedo texture for a temperate mountain woodland slope. Orthographic straight top-down view of grey-brown weathered rock fragments embedded in gritty muted brown soil, fine gravel, a few larger irregular stratified stone fragments, faint lichen, almost no vegetation. Stones range from small chips to palm-sized fragments, irregular natural distribution without obvious repeating shapes. Even neutral diffuse illumination, no directional shadows, no perspective, no vignette, no text, no borders. All four edges should tile seamlessly. Detailed physically believable surface, restrained natural colours, not shiny and not dramatic.
