# Additional meadow grass sources

Generated with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-10-28. Original transparent PNGs are kept in `public/textures/grass-upright.png` and `public/textures/grass-fine.png` (1254 × 1254 each). No source image resizing, recolouring or background replacement was applied. The browser derives normal maps, alpha-aware mipmaps, material coverage and linear colour profiles, then resamples these into the existing 1024-pixel atlas slots during baking.

These fill slots 10 and 11 in the existing four-column, three-row 4096 × 3072 grass atlas. Atlas dimensions, mesh count and draw structure stay unchanged. There are two additional source downloads and cached normal/mipmap records; source storage and the first bake are not free. After the coverage regression in v9, v10 restores the complete original tuft population and generates the new plants in a separate seeded pass with 25% additional candidates. This increases near instance/buffer and overdraw cost; it does not add a material or draw group. The same generator feeds worker generation, L0/L1 cards and the L2/ground captures, while normalized combined proportions feed far colour and voxel material lookup. Upright blades now span 0.85–1.45m tall; spreading grass spans 0.45–0.85m before habitat scaling.

## Upright grass prompt

Create one photorealistic game foliage texture asset, square 1024x1024 composition, actual transparent alpha background. A single sparse upright tuft of temperate meadow grass, loose irregular growth, about 18 slender slightly curved green blades with a few branching delicate seed stems, uneven heights, asymmetrical airy silhouette with large transparent gaps between individual blades, no dense bushy ball, no dome. Front orthographic side view, roots at horizontal centre near bottom 95 percent, longest blades reach near 8 percent from top, whole plant fits within image with transparent margin. Natural muted medium olive green, a few straw coloured tips and stems. Diffuse neutral even illumination suitable for unlit albedo; fine botanical vein detail, sharp clean alpha edge, no cast shadow, no ground, no soil, no rock, no pot, no text, no frame, no checkerboard backdrop. This will be used on crossed billboard cards in a realistic woodland renderer. Do not include several separate plants, one loosely connected tuft.

## Spreading fine grass prompt

Create one photorealistic game foliage texture asset, square 1024x1024 composition, actual transparent alpha background. A single low spreading tuft of fine temperate woodland-edge grass, about 24 thin flexible long blades arching outward in different directions, some blades almost horizontal near the bottom, sparse irregular wispy asymmetrical open silhouette with clearly visible transparent gaps throughout. A different growth habit from a dense rounded clump: three small offset growth points joined at the narrow base, one side droops more than the other, varied blade lengths. Front orthographic side view. The entire foliage occupies most of the square with clear transparent margin; roots lie at centre bottom around 95 percent, highest arch at 12 percent from top. Natural muted soft green with subtle yellow-green new growth, a few brown older blades at base. Diffuse neutral even illumination for albedo, fine sharp botanical detail. No baked highlights or cast shadow, no earth or ground plane, no rocks, no pot, no text, no frame, no checkerboard. Actual transparent cutout for crossed billboard grass cards.
